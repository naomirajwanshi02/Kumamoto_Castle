# Sources

Facts in `js/data.js` and the proportions in `js/castle.js`:

- Built by Katō Kiyomasa, begun 1601, main keep finished 1607.
- Main keep (Daitenshu): looks like three storeys, holds six floors above ground plus one basement; about 30 m high.
  Small keep (Shotenshu): about 19 m high, joined to the main keep. Exterior of black boards (shitami-ita) with white plaster gables.
  - [Kumamoto Castle official guide, About](https://castle.kumamoto-guide.jp/en/about/)
  - [MLIT: The Rebuilt Keeps](https://www.mlit.go.jp/tagengo-db/common/001550683.docx)
- Curved stone walls called musha-gaeshi ("warrior returning"); iron spikes (shinobi-gaeshi) above the small keep's wall.
- Keeps burned in 1877 (Satsuma Rebellion); present keeps are a 1960 reinforced-concrete rebuilding; damaged in the 2016 earthquakes, restoration completed March 2021.
  - [Obayashi: Design Stories](https://www.obayashi.co.jp/en/thinking/detail/focus18.html)
  - [Kumamoto Castle (Wikipedia)](https://www.wikipedia.com/wiki/Kumamoto_Castle)

The castle illustration used as the tracking picture was supplied for this project. The 3D model is an original, procedural drawing based on photographs and the descriptions above, not a scan.
